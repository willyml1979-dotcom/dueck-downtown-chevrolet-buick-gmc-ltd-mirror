Introducing the stunning black 2026 GMC Sierra 1500 SLT, a new Crew Cab Short Box truck with 4-wheel drive that is ready to conquer any terrain. Powered by a robust 6.2L EcoTec3 V8 engine, this versatile vehicle delivers 420 horsepower at 5600 RPM and is equipped with a 10-speed automatic transmission that includes a tow/haul mode and steering column paddle shifters.

The Sierra 1500 SLT stands out with its 18" machined aluminum wheels with dark grey metallic accents and all-terrain blackwall tires. Its Onyx Black exterior seamlessly complements its spacious Jet Black perforated leather interior, which offers top-notch comfort with features such as front bucket seats and a floor-mounted centre console.

Inside, you'll find advanced technology including a 13.4" diagonal Premium GMC infotainment system with Google Built-In apps, Bluetooth streaming, and wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay compatibility. This model also boasts a Bose Premium Audio System with seven speakers including a Richbass woofer, creating an immersive listening experience.

Safety and convenience are paramount with the ProGrade Trailering System featuring Hitch View and an In-Vehicle Trailering App, adaptive cruise control, lane assist, and a backup camera. Drivers will appreciate the ease of remote start, keyless entry, and a heated steering wheel for those chilly mornings. Additionally, the SLT package includes ventilated front seats, a wireless charging pad, power raking and telescoping steering column, and more.

This GMC Sierra 1500 SLT is perfect for those who demand both luxury and capability in a truck. Whether you're towing, off-roading, or commuting, it offers the features needed to enhance every drive.
