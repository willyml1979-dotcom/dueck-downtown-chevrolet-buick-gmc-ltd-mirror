The 2025 GMC Sierra 1500 Denali Ultimate in striking silver with a 4x4 drivetrain offers superior handling and capability for those who demand the best in a truck. This Crew Cab Short Box model is designed to impress with both its powerful presence and luxurious features.

The exquisite exterior boasts running boards and heated mirrors, while the rich brown interior features premium leather seats and a leather steering wheel, ensuring every journey is traveled in style and comfort. Standard on the Denali Ultimate, heated and cooled seats, along with a heated steering wheel, add to an exclusive comfort experience.

This Sierra comes equipped with cutting-edge technology, including a state-of-the-art navigation system, a heads-up display, and a comprehensive backup camera, parking sensors, and lane assist for enhanced safety and convenience. The smart device integration, Wi-Fi hotspot, and Sirius XM with a 3-year subscription ensure you stay connected wherever you go.

Under the hood, you'll find an impressive Duramax 3.0L Turbo-Diesel I6 engine, delivering 305 hp at 3750 rpm and a substantial 495 lb-ft of torque, paired with a 10-speed automatic transmission featuring electronic precision shift and tow/haul mode. Features like cruise grade and powertrain grade braking enhance control and performance.
